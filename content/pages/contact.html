Getting Here

Transit and parking

450 Sutter Street sits in the middle of downtown San Francisco, so most patients arrive on foot or by rail. If you drive, the closest garage is a few steps from our entrance.

directions_transit

By BART and Muni

The nearest rail station is BART's Montgomery St. Station at 598 Market Street, in the heart of the Financial District with onward connections to San Francisco Muni.[1] From there it is a short walk north to Sutter Street.

local_parking

By car

The closest public garage is the SFMTA Sutter-Stockton Garage at 444 Stockton Street, open 24 hours a few steps from our door.[2] Rates run from $6 per hour with a $40 maximum for up to 12 hours.[2]

Urgent Care

What to do in a dental emergency

A dental emergency is any problem needing prompt attention: severe toothache, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, uncontrolled bleeding, swelling, or a lost filling with a sharp edge. Call our 24/7 hotline at (800) 586-4621 first and describe what is happening, so we can reserve the right appointment for you.

If a permanent tooth is knocked out, keep it moist: place it back in the socket without touching the root, or keep it between your cheek and gum or in milk, then get to a dentist right away.[4] For a toothache, rinse with warm water and gently floss away trapped food, but never put aspirin on the gum.[4] See emergency dentistry for how we provide same-day relief.
