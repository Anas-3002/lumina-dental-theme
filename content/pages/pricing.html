Assurance

The No-Surprise-Bill Assurance

Under the federal No Surprises Act, patients who pay without insurance have a right to a good faith estimate of charges before care.

“If you’re uninsured or you decide not to use your health insurance for a service, under these protections, you can often get a good faith estimate of the cost of your care up front, before your visit.”

Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services[3]

Lumina goes further than the minimum. Every plan is issued in writing with each procedure and its code, you sign it before we begin, and if we find additional work mid-appointment we stop and re-quote rather than adding charges later. Federal rules let self-pay patients dispute a bill that lands at least $400 above their written estimate.[4] Our internal promise is simpler: the estimate you approve is the bill you receive. Restorations carry a 5-Year Restoration Warranty, and the 100% Pain-Free Promise applies to every visit.
