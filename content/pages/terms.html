Using this website

The information on this website is general dental information for residents of San Francisco and the wider Bay Area. It is not a diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for an examination by a licensed dentist who can see your teeth, your radiographs and your medical history. No dentist–patient relationship is created by reading this site or by sending an appointment request.

If you think you have a dental emergency, call (800) 586-4621 rather than relying on the website. If you have facial swelling that is affecting your breathing or swallowing, or a suspected jaw fracture, go to an emergency department.

Appointment requests

Submitting the booking form is a request, not a confirmed appointment. An appointment exists once a member of our team has agreed a specific time with you and confirmed it in writing. Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if you need to move or cancel an appointment so the clinical time can be offered to another patient. Repeated late cancellations or non-attendance may lead to a fee that reflects the reserved clinical time, and we will always tell you before applying it.

Examinations, treatment plans and consent

Every course of treatment begins with an examination. We provide a written treatment plan setting out the options, the recommended sequence and the price of each item before treatment starts. Complex treatments — implant surgery, sedation, full-arch reconstruction, orthodontics — require separate written consent, and you will be given time to ask questions and to take the plan away for a second opinion if you wish. You may decline or stop treatment at any point.

Our guarantees

  • 100% Pain-Free Promise. If you experience pain during a procedure that you have not agreed to, tell us at the time. We stop, and we make it right — including completing the work at no additional professional fee.
  • Five-year restoration warranty. Crowns, bridges, veneers and implant-supported restorations placed by us are warranted for five years against failure of our workmanship, provided you attend the recommended maintenance appointments and follow the home-care advice we give you. The warranty does not cover trauma, untreated grinding, smoking-related complications, or a failure to attend maintenance.

This warranty is given in addition to your statutory consumer rights, which it does not replace or restrict.

Fees, insurance and payment

Prices published on this website are starting fees for this practice and are confirmed in writing after examination. Where an estimate changes because treatment turns out to be more complex than the examination suggested, we stop and re-plan with you before continuing. Payment is due on the day of treatment unless we have agreed a payment plan in writing. We accept cash, major cards, CareCredit and HSA/FSA cards, and we will submit claims to your dental plan where we are in network.

Responsibility for the account rests with the patient, not the insurer, including where a claim is denied. We will tell you promptly if a claim is rejected so you can decide how to proceed.

  • Arrive on time. If you are running late, call us — we will tell you honestly whether we can still complete the treatment in the time available.
