What happens next

  1. 1

    We confirm your slot

    A coordinator texts or calls to agree a specific time and confirms it in writing. If the time you asked for is full, they will offer the nearest alternatives.

  2. 2

    You come in for the examination

    Allow 60 to 75 minutes. You will have a full examination, low-dose digital X-rays, a 3D CBCT scan where it is clinically indicated, and a periodontal and soft-tissue check.

  3. 3

    You leave with a written plan and a fixed price

    Options in plain English, what each one costs, and the order we would recommend. Nothing is booked in or charged without your agreement.

Before you come in

  • Bring photo ID and your insurance card if you have cover.
  • Have a list of your medications and any medical conditions ready.
  • Tell us if you are anxious — it changes how we plan the appointment.
