Clinical Leadership

Who leads your care

Every treatment plan is signed off by a clinical lead, so a specialist, a hygienist and a coordinator are never working from different pictures of your mouth. You should never have to repeat your story to a different chair.

star

Dr. Sarah Jenkins, DDS

Clinical Lead · Board-Certified

Dr. Jenkins leads diagnosis, smile design and treatment planning for complex cosmetic and restorative cases. She sets the clinical standards for the practice, reviews every plan that involves veneers, implants or full-mouth reconstruction, and is the clinician most new patients meet at their first consultation. She also mentors the hygiene team on minimally invasive, anxiety-aware care.

Specialist Leads

The specialists behind the smile

When your plan needs a specialist, you see one in house rather than being sent across town. Each lead below works alongside Dr. Jenkins on your case, and you keep the same lead from consultation through follow-up.

medical_services

Dr. Ethan Cole, DDS, MS

Implant & Surgical Lead

Plans and places single implants, bridges and full-arch restorations using guided surgery. Reviews every bone grafting and grafting-free case before an implant is placed, then stages the sequence with the restorative lead.

child_care

Dr. Priya Raman, DDS, MSD

Orthodontic Lead

Directs clear aligner therapy and bite correction, including Invisalign cases run alongside veneers. Maps your movement plan digitally so you can see the projected result before you start, and adjusts it as your teeth move.

health_and_safety

Dr. Nathan Brooks, DDS

Sedation & Anxiety Care Lead

Assesses anxious patients and plans comfort options, from nitrous oxide to oral sedation. Sets the pacing of longer procedures so nobody feels rushed or overwhelmed, and briefs the wider team on what helps you stay calm.

Hygiene & Coordination

The team you see most often

Hygienists and coordinators carry most of your appointments. They keep your preventive care on schedule and your questions answered, and you will usually meet them before you meet the dentist.

clean_hands

Renée Alvarez, RDH

Lead Dental Hygienist

Delivers preventive care and periodontal maintenance, and builds the cleaning and recall routine that keeps your gums stable between visits. She also coaches you on the home care that makes each cleaning last.

dentistry

Marcus Boyd, RDH

Hygienist & Preventive Care

Handles new-patient cleanings, sealants and fluoride, and takes the intraoral scans and X-rays that feed your baseline record. He is usually the first clinician to welcome nervous new patients.

support_agent

Dana Whitfield

Patient Care Coordinator

Schedules your visits, verifies insurance, prepares written estimates and walks you through payment options before treatment begins. She is your first call for anything administrative.

Working Together

How our team works together

Your case is reviewed as a team, not handed off in isolation. When a plan combines specialties, the implant lead and orthodontic lead work with Dr. Jenkins on the same roadmap so the sequence of treatment is planned from the start. Read more about our dental implant and Invisalign services, or see how we support anxious patients with sedation and anxiety-free dentistry.

If you are unsure which clinician you need, the coordinator routes your request to the right lead. Meet the people who will look after you by booking an appointment, or read the patient stories from the people they have treated. We will always tell you in advance who you are seeing and why.
