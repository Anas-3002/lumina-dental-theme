How to choose: match the symptom to the treatment

Most patients arrive describing a symptom rather than a treatment name. This is the mapping we use in the clinic.

What you notice Likely cause Treatment group
Gums bleed when brushing, persistent bad breath Gingivitis, or periodontitis if there is bone loss Preventive & hygiene
One tooth missing, or a denture that moves Previous extraction or tooth loss Dental implants
Teeth look short, chipped, stained or uneven Enamel wear, developmental shape, intrinsic staining Veneers or whitening
Crowding, gaps or a bite that has shifted Malocclusion Invisalign
A chipped or heavily filled tooth that hurts when biting Fracture or failing restoration Same-day crown

Why prevention comes first

The most useful treatment for most new patients is not the most expensive one. Tooth decay (caries) and periodontitis are the two leading causes of tooth loss in adults, and both are largely preventable and treatable before they reach that point.[3]

The prevalence data is blunt. About 4 in 10 US adults aged 30 or older have some level of periodontitis, rising to about 6 in 10 among adults aged 65 and over.[1] Among adults aged 65 or older, one quarter have eight or fewer teeth, which is the threshold at which chewing meat, fruit and vegetables becomes genuinely difficult.[4]

"Dental caries (tooth decay) and periodontal (gum) disease are leading causes of tooth loss... Personal, professional, and population-based strategies to prevent and control gum disease and tooth decay can help prevent tooth loss."

— Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, Oral Health

That is why every treatment plan at Lumina starts with an examination rather than a procedure. If you need hygiene care and a filling, that is what we will quote you — not a full smile makeover. Where a cosmetic treatment genuinely makes sense, the technology we use means you can see the projected result before committing.
