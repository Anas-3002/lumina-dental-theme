Book Your Appointment

Send the form and a clinical coordinator will text you within 10 minutes during opening hours with priority slots. Prefer to speak to someone? Call (800) 586-4621 — the line is answered 24 hours a day, including weekends, for dental emergencies.

What happens next

  1. 1 We confirm within 10 minutes. A coordinator texts or calls you during opening hours to agree a time.
  2. 2 You get a written plan. Exam, low-dose X-rays, 3D CBCT scan where indicated, and a fixed price before treatment starts.
  3. 3 You decide. Nothing is booked in or charged without your agreement, and you can take the plan home.

Bring or have ready

  • Photo ID and your insurance card, if you have cover.
  • A list of medications and any relevant medical conditions.
  • Details of previous dental treatment, if you have them (we can request records).
  • Any dental anxiety or past trauma — tell us, it changes how we plan the visit.
