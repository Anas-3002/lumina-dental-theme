Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully. Effective 6 October 2026.

Our legal duty

Lumina Dental Studio is required by law to maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information (PHI), to give you this notice explaining our legal duties and privacy practices, and to notify you following a breach of unsecured PHI. We must follow the terms of the notice currently in effect.

How we may use and disclose your health information

We may use and disclose your health information for the following purposes without your written authorisation:

  • Treatment. To provide, coordinate and manage your dental care — for example sharing a radiograph with the specialist placing your implant, or sending a prescription to your pharmacy.
  • Payment. To bill and be paid for treatment, including sending claims to your dental insurer, verifying benefits and, where you have asked us to, contacting you about balances.
  • Health care operations. To run the practice: quality assessment, clinical audits, staff training, licensing and accreditation, and business planning.
  • Appointment reminders and treatment alternatives. To remind you of appointments by text, phone or email and to tell you about treatment options or related services we think may be relevant.
  • As required by law. For example in response to a court order, a public health reporting requirement, or a lawful request from a health oversight agency.

Uses that need your written authorisation

We will not use or disclose your health information for marketing, for the sale of your information, or for most disclosures of psychotherapy notes without your written authorisation. If you give authorisation, you may revoke it in writing at any time, and we will stop using or disclosing information from that point forward.

Your rights

  • Right to inspect and copy. You may ask to see and obtain a copy of your dental and billing records. We will respond within 30 days. A reasonable, cost-based fee may apply for copies and postage.
  • Right to amend. If you believe information in your record is incorrect or incomplete, you may ask us to amend it. We may deny the request in limited circumstances and will explain why in writing.
  • Right to an accounting of disclosures. You may request a list of disclosures we have made of your health information, other than for treatment, payment, health care operations and certain other purposes. The first list you request within a 12-month period is free.
  • Right to request restrictions. You may ask us not to use or disclose particular information for treatment, payment or operations. We are not required to agree, except where you ask us not to disclose information to your health plan for an item or service you paid for in full out of pocket.
  • Right to confidential communications. You may ask us to contact you at a specific phone number or address. We will accommodate reasonable requests, and you do not have to explain why.
  • Right to a paper copy. You may ask for a paper copy of this notice at any time, even if you have agreed to receive it electronically.

How to exercise your rights

Contact our Privacy Officer in writing at Lumina Dental Studio, 450 Sutter St, Suite 1800, San Francisco, CA 94108, or email hello@luminadentalstudio.com, or call (800) 586-4621. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the practice or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

Recording of phone calls

Our 24/7 emergency line may be answered by a clinical triage service. Calls are not recorded. If we ever wish to record a call for training, we will ask your permission first and you may decline.

Website forms

Information submitted through the appointment forms on this website is used to contact you about the appointment you requested. Do not send clinical details, imaging or insurance documents through the website forms. Once you become a patient, that information forms part of your record and is covered by this notice.

Changes to this notice

We reserve the right to change this notice and to make the revised notice effective for all health information we already have about you as well as any information we receive in the future. The current version is always posted at /hipaa-notice/ with its effective date, and a copy is available at reception.

This notice is provided as part of a demonstration practice website. Before using it for a real practice, have it reviewed by your compliance adviser so that it matches your actual workforce, business associates and state-law obligations.

Want your records, or a correction?

Our Privacy Officer responds to records requests within 30 days. Call or email and we will send you the short request form.
