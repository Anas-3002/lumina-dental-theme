Privacy Policy

How Lumina Dental Studio collects, uses and protects information submitted through this website. Last reviewed and updated 6 October 2026.

Who we are

Lumina Dental Studio is a dental practice at 450 Sutter St, Suite 1800, San Francisco, CA 94108. We are the data controller for information collected through this website. You can reach our privacy contact at hello@luminadentalstudio.com or on (800) 586-4621.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the website at luminadentalstudio.com. It does not cover the clinical records we hold about you as a patient, which are governed by our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices and by California law. If you are already a patient, the HIPAA notice is the document that applies to your health information.

What we collect

  • Appointment requests. When you submit a booking or contact form we collect the name, phone number, email address, treatment interest, coverage preference, preferred time and any message you write.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting platform records server logs including IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs are used for security and capacity, not for advertising.
  • Cookies. This site sets only the cookies needed to operate it — for example a session cookie when you log in to the practice area. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

Why we use it

We use appointment-request information for one purpose: to contact you about the appointment you asked for, to prepare for your visit, and to keep a record of the request. We do not sell it, rent it, or pass it to advertisers. Information is shared only with service providers who help us operate the practice — for example our hosting provider and, where applicable, our practice management software — and only to the extent needed to provide that service.

How long we keep it

Appointment requests that do not become patient records are kept for 24 months and then deleted. If you become a patient, your clinical record is kept for the period required by California law and the applicable dental record-retention rules, and is governed by the HIPAA notice.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what we hold about you and request a copy.
  • You can ask us to correct anything inaccurate.
  • You can ask us to delete an appointment request that did not become a patient record.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you about appointments at any time.

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to request deletion. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of on that point. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@luminadentalstudio.com and we will respond within 45 days.

Security

Form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in systems accessible only to authorised practice staff. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive clinical details, imaging or insurance documents through the website forms — bring those to your appointment or send them through a channel we have agreed with you.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging their own or a family member's care. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through the website. Where a child is a patient, their record is created and held with parental consent.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page and change the review date at the top. Material changes affecting patient records will be communicated under the HIPAA notice.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@luminadentalstudio.com, or call the practice on (800) 586-4621.

This policy is written for a demonstration dental practice website. Before going live with real patient data, have it reviewed against your practice's actual systems, sub-processors and record-retention obligations.
